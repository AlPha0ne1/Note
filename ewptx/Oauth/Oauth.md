Let’s say you want to log into a website (e.g., an online app like "PhotoStorage") using your Google account.

You visit PhotoStorage and click the “Login with Google” button.

PhotoStorage redirects you to Google where you log in and grant permission to share your email and profile info with PhotoStorage.

Google sends an authorization code back to PhotoStorage.

PhotoStorage exchanges the authorization code for an access token by making a request to Google's token endpoint.

PhotoStorage uses the access token to retrieve your profile info from Google's API.

You are logged into PhotoStorage with your Google account details.
