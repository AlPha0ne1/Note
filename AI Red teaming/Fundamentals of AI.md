Q:Which probabilistic algorithm, based on Bayes' theorem, is commonly used for classification tasks such as 
spam filtering and sentiment analysis, and is known for its simplicity, efficiency, and good performance in real-world scenarios?

Ans:Naive Bayes

## Spam Filtering 

Spam filtering is the process of automatically detecting and separating unwanted, harmful, or junk messages from legitimate communication </br>
Example:
```
Routing emails about "free lottery prizes" directly to the spam folder.
Blocking phishing messages that pretend to be from your bank.
Hiding automated bot comments on social media posts.
```

## Sentiment analysis (opinion mining)

Sentiment analysis (also called opinion mining) is the process of identifying the emotional tone behind a body of text. </br>
Example:
```
Tracking customer reviews to see if people like a new smartphone.
Monitoring social media to gauge public reaction to a movie release.
Analyzing customer service chats to flag angry users for immediate help.
```
